Pip Garden is a focus timer that grows a garden on your device. This policy explains
what the app does with information about you. The short version: **nothing leaves your
device.**

## What the app does not do

- **No account.** You never sign up, sign in or give a name, email or phone number.
- **No server.** The app does not talk to any server, ours or anyone else's. On Android it
  does not request permission to use the internet; on iOS it makes no network requests.
- **No advertising, no tracking, no analytics services.** There are no third-party SDKs for
  ads, analytics, crash reporting or attribution in the app.
- **No selling or sharing.** We have no data about you to sell or share, and we do not
  receive any.
- **No purchases.** The app is free and contains nothing to buy.

## What the app keeps, on your device only

To work, the app stores the following in a private database on your phone:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your focus sessions: when each started, how long it was meant to run, whether it was completed, how long it was paused | To grow your garden and show your journal |
| Your garden: each plant, where it stands, when it was planted; any decor you placed | So your garden is still there tomorrow |
| Your settings: session and break lengths, sounds, theme, switches | So the app remembers your choices |
| A local log of how the app is used (for example, "a session was started", "a setting was changed") | So the app's behaviour can be understood during development. It stays on your device, is limited in size and old entries are deleted automatically, and it is never sent anywhere |

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it.

## Sharing your garden

If you choose **Export**, the app makes a picture of your garden and opens your phone's own
share sheet (or saves it to your photos on iOS, after asking your permission). Where the
picture goes after that is up to you and the app you share it with. The picture contains
only your garden.

## Permissions the app asks for

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To tell you when a session or a break ends while the app is in the background, and to show the timer on your lock screen / in your notification shade while a session runs |
| Exact alarms (Android) | So the "session complete" notification arrives at the exact moment your session ends |
| Run after restart (Android) | So a session running when your phone restarts is still announced |
| Foreground service (Android) | To show the running timer in an ongoing notification |
| Do Not Disturb access (Android, optional) | Only if you turn on Quiet mode: the app silences your phone during a session and restores it afterwards |
| Vibration | For the gentle tap when a session ends, if haptics are on |
| Network state (Android) | Requested automatically by the audio component the app uses. The app has no internet permission and does not go online |
| Add to Photos (iOS) | Only when you choose to save a picture of your garden |

## Deleting your data

- **In the app:** Settings → Reset garden deletes every plant, session, decor item and the
  local usage log. Your settings are kept.
- **Completely:** uninstall the app. Everything it stored is removed with it.

Because nothing is stored anywhere but your device, there is nothing for us to delete on our
side.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
